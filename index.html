About Model

Experimental respiratory sound classifier — research prototype.

Extra Trees Classifier

An ensemble of randomized decision trees that scores each 2-second audio segment using 264 extracted acoustic features. Feature extraction and classification run entirely in the Python backend.

Feature Extraction

  • 40 MFCC coefficients (mean & std dev)
  • Delta and delta-delta MFCC features
  • Spectral centroid, bandwidth, rolloff
  • Zero-crossing rate and RMS energy
  • Spectral contrast features

Audio Processing

Limitations & Disclaimer

These are experimental held-out test-set results on the Kaggle Respiratory Sound Database. False positives occur, and these metrics are not evidence of clinical reliability. WheezeDetect is a research prototype, not a medical diagnostic device. Its output should never replace professional medical assessment.
